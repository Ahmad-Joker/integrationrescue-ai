# Interview outreach messages

Templates for Ahmad to send personally. Rules: send them from your own accounts, make no claim of customers or
pilots, never ask for real customer data, and use the consent text before taking notes. Replace `[…]` fields;
`[your email]` stays empty until you choose a public contact address.

## 1. Short LinkedIn connection note (≤ 300 characters)
> Hi [first name], I'm Ahmad, an AI student at AAST building an agent that diagnoses "customer paid but didn't get
> access" billing-integration failures. I'm not selling anything. Could I ask you 3 questions about how your team
> handles these? 20 minutes, whenever suits you.

## 2. Longer LinkedIn message
> Hi [first name],
>
> I'm Ahmad Essam Abdelaziz, a BSc Artificial Intelligence student at AAST and an AI-agent developer. I'm building
> IntegrationRescue, a prototype agent that investigates silent integration failures (for example, a customer pays,
> billing says succeeded, the webhook returns 202, but the product still shows the free plan), tests a fix in a
> sandbox, and only calls it fixed after an independent check.
>
> I'm trying to learn whether this problem is real and costly for teams like yours at [company], before building
> further. Would you have 20 minutes for a call? I'm not selling anything: no pitch until the very end, and only if
> you want to see the prototype. Everything runs on fictional data.
>
> Thanks either way,
> Ahmad

## 4. Warm-introduction request (to the person who knows them)
> Hi [mutual contact], would you be comfortable introducing me to [name] at [company]? I'm researching how SaaS
> teams handle "paid but no access" billing-integration incidents for my AI agent project. It's a 20-minute
> conversation, no sales, and I'd ask about their experience only. Here's a two-line blurb you can forward:
>
> *"Ahmad (AI student, AAST) is building an agent that diagnoses and verifies fixes for billing → product-access
> integration failures. He'd like 20 minutes to learn how your team handles these. No pitch, no data needed."*

## 5. Follow-up after five days
> Hi [first name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Even 15 minutes would help a lot, or a pointer to whoever
> handles billing or webhook incidents on your team. Thanks, Ahmad

## 6. Interview invitation (after a yes)
> Thanks, [first name]! Here's a 20-minute slot: [date/time, time zone], [video link].
> Agenda: your role and stack (3 min) · the last billing/integration incident you remember (10 min) · what you'd need
> to trust an automated fix (5 min) · optional 2-minute demo at the end.
> I'll take anonymised notes (no names or company names in anything I publish). Is that OK?

## 7. Consent text (read at the start of the call)
> "Before we start: I'll take notes, anonymised. Your name and company won't appear anywhere public; I'll describe
> you only by role, company size band and region. I'll only quote you, anonymously, if you say yes now. You can skip
> any question or stop at any time. Is that OK? And may I quote you anonymously?"

Record both answers (notes yes/no, quotes yes/no) in the tracker before asking anything else.
