# Pitch recording checklist (six minutes, face visible)

**Setup**
- [ ] Camera at eye level, face well lit from the front (window or lamp), plain background
- [ ] External or headset microphone; quiet room; 10-second test, listen back
- [ ] Recording layout: slides full screen with your face as picture-in-picture (OBS Studio scene), or camera only
      with slides inserted in editing. **Your face must be visible throughout 2:00–6:00**
- [ ] Slides exported from the deck (PITCH_DECK.md / .pptx) at 1920×1080
- [ ] The two-minute demo video ready to place at 0:00–2:00

**Content check (before recording)**
- [ ] Every number on the slides appears in TECHNICAL_EVIDENCE.md, with live and baseline kept separate
- [ ] ⚠ lines in PITCH_SCRIPT_6_MIN.md: real evidence inserted, or the fallback sentence used
- [ ] No mention of customers, pilots, revenue or market size without evidence
- [ ] Limitations sentence included (simulated systems, fictional data, prototype)

**Recording**
- [ ] Three timed rehearsals; final take 5:45–6:00, hard maximum 6:00
- [ ] Speak to the lens, not the screen; pause after each number
- [ ] English narration; record the presentation in one or two takes, not sentence by sentence

**Editing and upload**
- [ ] Demo (0:00–2:00) + presentation (2:00–6:00) joined; total ≤ 6:00
- [ ] English captions (auto-generate, then correct names: IntegrationRescue, Gemini, webhook)
- [ ] Audio levels even between demo and presentation; no music over speech
- [ ] Upload **Unlisted**; test the link in a private window; record the link in SUBMISSION_READINESS.md
- [ ] Watch the final upload once from start to end
